How detection works

01 — CAPTURE

Frame ingestion

Live camera or uploaded footage is read frame by frame, in real time or on demand.

02 — ANALYZE

Dual-model inference

Each frame runs through a weapon detector and a motion-based violence classifier in parallel.

03 — ALERT

Instant flagging

Detections are overlaid on the feed immediately with a visible, timestamped alert.
